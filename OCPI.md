## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

The **One Community Platform Initiative (OCPI)** is a pan-African non-governmental and civic development organization committed to inclusive grassroots empowerment, socioeconomic transformation, educational technology adoption, and community self-reliance across Nigeria and Africa.

This document formalizes the Information Architecture (IA), functional navigation, page-level hierarchy, content models, and digital ecosystem requirements based on the official OCPI Master Site Map.

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives & Core Themes

1. **Social Development:** Community emergency response, skill acquisition, minority integration, and accredited fellowship programs.
2. **Social Enterprise & Tech Inclusion:** Sustainable civic solutions, grassroots technology education, and collaborative community open labs.
3. **Research & Knowledge Management (JRG - Joint Research Group):** Action-oriented research across agricultural, educational, urban, youth, and food security domains.
4. **Governance & Transparency:** Clear documentation of executive leadership, governing council, financial sustainability audits, and donor partnerships.
5. **Community Mobilization & Engagement:** Multiple on-ramps for membership, volunteerism, fellowships, institutional partnerships, and donor contributions.

## 5. Technical & User Experience (UX) Recommendations

### 5.1. UI/UX Strategy
1. **Design Theme:** Sleek modern civic aestheticâ€”deep navy/slate foundation (`#0f172a`), vibrant emerald/green accent for grassroots growth (`#10b981`), warm gold/amber for accolades and community warmth (`#f59e0b`).
2. **Mega-Menu Navigation:** Given the 11+ main sections and dozens of sub-pages, the desktop header should feature structured mega-menus grouped clearly by intent (Governance, What We Do, Research/JRG, Media/Press, Get Involved).
3. **Mobile-First Responsive Drawer:** Accordion-based drawer navigation ensuring all nested board, research, and membership subcategories remain discoverable with zero friction on mobile devices.
4. **Interactive JRG Research Portal:** Searchable filter by Research Unit, publication year, and author campus.
5. **Membership Application Multi-step Wizard:** Unified form with branch logic (Partner vs Career vs Volunteer vs Studio Tech vs Ambassador) to streamline onboarding without form fatigue.

### 5.2. Proposed Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** Modern responsive web application (HTML5, Tailwind CSS / Vanilla CSS modern tokens, or Next.js / Vite + React).
* **Content Management / Backend:** Headless CMS or Supabase backend for managing news, events, JRG research papers, team directory (28 board members, 11 research staff, 7 admin), and membership form submissions.
* **Form Handling & Automation:** Automated email confirmations for membership submissions, event calendar ICS download, and receipt generation for donors.
